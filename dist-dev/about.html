About Peacock Technotrics.

A software studio with over two decades of specialized experience in Diamond & Jewellery ERP development — the premier technology partner for manufacturers, traders, and enterprises across the global gem and jewellery industry.

We partner with clients, not just deliver to them.

For over two decades, Peacock Technotrics has stood as the premier technology partner for manufacturers, traders, and enterprises across the global gem and jewellery industry. Our specialized focus on Diamond & Jewellery ERP development has shaped a practice that understands not just software — but the business of gems.

We possess a profound, end-to-end understanding of the diamond manufacturing journey — from rough stone procurement through cutting, polishing, certification, and final product delivery. This depth allows us to architect ERP solutions that genuinely address the unique complexities and regulatory demands of this highly specialized industry, rather than forcing generic software onto a craft it was never built for.

We go beyond traditional software delivery. We partner strategically with our clients to orchestrate comprehensive digital transformation — optimizing operational workflows, unlocking efficiency, and accelerating sustainable business growth through intelligent, enterprise-grade technology platforms. Our clients don’t just receive software; they gain a long-term technology partner invested in their success.
