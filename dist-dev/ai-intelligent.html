The definition

A second pair of hands. Not a second headcount.

AI is software that learns a pattern from your own records, then applies it to the next one. It does not know your business by default. We teach it your formats, your grades and your terms, then keep a person at the gate.

How we work

One workflow. Then the next.

We do not start with a platform. We start with the task your team repeats most, prove it on your data, and wire it into the ERP only when the output is trusted.

01
Discovery

Find the task

We spend time on the floor and in the ledger. We pick the workflow with a clear input, a clear output and a visible cost when it is done by hand.

02
Build

Build the loop

Model, rules and a review screen. Trained on your records, tested against last quarter, including the ugly scans and the missing fields.

03
Live

Put it in the ERP

Same roles, same fields, same audit trail. Your team learns one screen, not a new system. We stay through the first close.

Then the next workflow.

No rip and replace. If you already run an ERP, we add to it. If you do not, we build the workflow and connect it.
